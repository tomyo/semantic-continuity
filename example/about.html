About Custom Elements in WPM

In the Web Progressive Materialization architecture, we distinguish between Enhanced Components and Autonomous Components.

  • Enhanced Components (`enhance.js`): Provide progressive behavior on top of materialized HTML. They use stateless event delegation and do not require registering a Custom Element.
  • Autonomous Components (`component-name.js`): Fully encapsulate their logic and markup. They use `customElements.define` because they generate or manage runtime-owned UI.

Look at the bottom right corner of this page. The <live-chat> widget is an example of an Autonomous Component.
